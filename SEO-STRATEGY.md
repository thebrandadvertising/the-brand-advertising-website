## Backlink and off-site framework

### Technical items

- Keep the canonical `.com` domain consistent across profiles and citations.
- Maintain an accurate sitemap and notify search engines after meaningful releases.
- Prepare shareable case-study URLs with clear titles and images.
- Keep organization name, phone, email and social links consistent.

### Human outreach

- Ask campaign clients for permission to link to published case studies.
- Request accurate listings from relevant advertising and business directories after verifying their quality.
- Seek mentions from event venues, retail partners, industry associations and campaign collaborators where a real relationship exists.
- Publish useful campaign evidence that trade publications can cite.
- Avoid paid link schemes, bulk directory submissions and fabricated reviews.
